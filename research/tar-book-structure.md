# TAR Book Structure

> TAR - Training and Assessment Record

## Candidate & Administrative Details

### 1. TAR Book Identification & Administration
- **TAR Book Serial / ID Number**
- **Date of Issue**
- **Issuing Authority**
  - Authority / Organization Name (MTI or Sponsoring Shipping Company)
  - Authorized Signatory Name & Designation
  - Issuing Official Signature & Official Stamp
- **Trainee Rank / Training Stream**
  - Stream (e.g., Deck Cadet / Nautical, Trainee Marine Engineer, Trainee ETO, GP Rating Trainee)

---

### 2. Candidate Personal Details
- **Photo** (Passport-size formal uniform/white background)
- **Full Legal Name** (Title, First Name, Middle Name, Surname)
- **Gender**
- **Date of Birth (DOB)**
- **Place of Birth** (City, State, Country)
- **Nationality / Citizenship**
- **Physical Identification**
  - Distinguishing Physical Marks / Scars
  - Height (cm) & Weight (kg)
  - Blood Group & Rh Factor
- **Candidate Specimen Signature**

---

### 3. Contact & Next of Kin (NOK) Information
- **Personal Contact Details**
  - Mobile Number / Alternate Contact
  - Primary Email Address
  - Permanent Residential Address
  - Current Mailing Address (if different)
- **Next of Kin / Emergency Contact**
  - Full Name
  - Relationship to Candidate
  - Residential Address
  - Contact Telephone / Mobile Number
  - Email Address

---

### 4. Identity & Travel Documents
- **INDOS Number** (Indian National Database of Seafarers / Flag State Seafarer Reference)
- **Continuous Discharge Certificate (CDC) / Seaman's Book**
  - CDC Number
  - Issuing Country / Flag State
  - Date of Issue & Place of Issue
  - Validity / Expiry Date
- **Passport Details**
  - Passport Number
  - Issuing Country
  - Date of Issue & Place of Issue
  - Validity / Expiry Date
- **Seafarers Identity Document (SID)**
  - SID Number (ILO Convention 185 Biometric ID)
  - Issuing Flag State & Expiry Date

---

### 5. Pre-Sea Education & Training Details
- **Maritime Training Institute (MTI)**
  - Name of the MTI
  - MTI Approval Number / DG Shipping Code
  - Campus Location / Address
- **Pre-Sea Program Information**
  - Course Name (e.g., DNS, B.Sc. Nautical Science, B.E./B.Tech Marine Engineering, GME, ETO, GP Rating)
  - University / Board Registration Number (e.g., IMU Registration No.)
  - Batch / Cadet Roll Number
  - Course Commencement Date
  - Course Completion / Passing Out Date
  - Course Completion Certificate Number & Issue Date

---

### 6. Mandatory STCW & Safety Certifications
- **Basic Safety Training (BST) Modular Certificates**
  - **PST** (Personal Survival Techniques): Certificate No., Issue Date, Expiry Date, Issuing MTI
  - **FPFF** (Fire Prevention & Fire Fighting): Certificate No., Issue Date, Expiry Date, Issuing MTI
  - **EFA** (Elementary First Aid): Certificate No., Issue Date, Expiry Date, Issuing MTI
  - **PSSR** (Personal Safety & Social Responsibilities): Certificate No., Issue Date, Expiry Date, Issuing MTI
- **Maritime Security Training**
  - **STSDSD** (Security Training for Seafarers with Designated Security Duties) or **SAT** (Security Awareness Training): Certificate No., Issue Date, Issuing MTI
- **Cargo / Specialized Modular Endorsements (if applicable)**
  - Basic Oil & Chemical Tanker Cargo Operations (OCTCO / OTFC / CTFC)
  - Basic Gas Tanker Cargo Operations (GTFC / LGFT)
  - Passenger Ship Familiarization / Crowd Management
- **Pre-Sea Medical Fitness Certificate**
  - Approval Flag / Body (e.g., DG Shipping Approved / MLC 2006 compliant)
  - Doctor Name & Medical Council Registration Number
  - Date of Examination / Issue Date
  - Certificate Validity / Expiry Date
  - Hearing, Color Vision & Visual Acuity Status

---

### 7. Sponsoring Company / RPSL Agency Details
- **Sponsoring Company / Ship Management Details**
  - Principal Company Name
  - Recruitment & Placement Services Licence (RPSL) Agency Name (if applicable)
  - RPSL / License Number (e.g., RPSL-MUM-xxx)
  - RPSL License Validity Date
  - Registered Head Office Address
- **Key Administrative Contacts**
  - Designated Person Ashore (DPA) Name & 24/7 Emergency Contact
  - Company Training Officer (CTO) / Cadets Coordinator Name
  - Official Telephone / Mobile Numbers
  - Official Contact Email Addresses
